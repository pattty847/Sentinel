Coinbase numbers l2, trades and heartbeats on one counter, so a reader that skips L2 frames (to avoid parsing ~90 % of the bytes) would drop every trade after the first. 4. **The primary `BookRecorder` path has the largest test blast radius and the parent plan assigns it to slice E** (pipeline section 9: `requestResnapshot` wiring, `recordingResnapshotRequested`, queue-overflow path "deleted at the end").
