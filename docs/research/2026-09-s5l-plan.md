# S5L plan: live edge for the GPU heatmap (owner-approved 2026-09-30)

Slice S5L of `2026-09-s5-plan.md` (section 7 item 4). The plan was drafted by a planning agent, reviewed by the orchestrator, and approved by the owner with the decisions in section 7.

## 1. Goal and scope

Stream the still-forming data at the live edge to the GPU path:

- the open minute;
- finished minutes still held by the recorder's lateness window;
- the forming bucket at the chart's timeframe.

With this, the GPU path keeps pace with the legacy recording chart, which updates at 1 Hz. Without it, the forming bucket draws the loading hatch. For timeframes above 1m, that means lagging by up to one whole bucket plus commit latency.

Out of scope:
- S6 (the main-chart switch) and S7 (labels and walls);
- any change to the hot paths `DataProcessor`, `HeatmapTwapStreamer` and `MainWindowGpu`;
- any change to the recorder.

**Source.** The source is the server's existing `LiveCache`, not `HeatmapTwapStreamer`:
- In recording mode, `DataProcessor` ignores `heatmap_slice` (`DataProcessor.cpp:136`). The legacy chart takes its live column from `heatmap_recording_live` (`MainWindowGpu.cpp:1025`).
- That message is fed by `BookRecorder`'s publisher into `LiveService` (`ServerDataModel.cpp:174-178`).
- `HeatmapTwapStreamer` samples the book itself on its own grid. Using it would mean a second integration of the book.

## 2. Wire and server (S5L-a)

**Messages** (JSON):
- `{"type":"heatmap_live_subscribe","sub":<u64>,"symbol":..,"sources":["hmc2.deep","hmc2.near"],"since_ms":<i64>}`
- `{"type":"heatmap_live_unsubscribe","symbol":..}`

Rules:
- `since_ms` is the client's `committedThroughMs` for the open chunk.
- Re-subscribing for a symbol replaces the old subscription.
- A session may subscribe to at most 8 symbols.
- Servers advertise support with `server_config.recording.chunk_live: true`.

**Frame:** the reserved SHC1 kind `LiveColumn` (2), one per source.
- The SHE1 request id carries `sub`.
- The key is the open minute's chunk.
- The state is open: `{committedThroughMs, revision}`, where `revision` is the `LiveCache` series revision. It is monotonic within one server process; a server restart may reset it.
- The payload is `SparseColumns` at the 1m level over `[tailStartMs, openMinuteEnd)`:
  - every provisional minute (pending plus open), with `kProvisional` set;
  - any committed finals this subscriber has not received yet.
- `scannedRanges` state that every recorder minute in that range is present.
- The extent may reach back at most one chunk, for a pending minute that straddles the hour.
- Each open-minute column carries `observedMs` (the covered time so far) and `kPartial`.

**Rate:** 1 Hz (owner decision 1). This follows `BookRecorder::publishOpen`'s 1000 ms throttle, which S5L does not change.

**Server side:** add "raw-tail" subscriptions to `LiveService`, next to the `LiveBuilder` subscriptions.
- They use the same worker, the same `LiveCadence` (1 s, backing off to 5 s), and final markers per subscriber.
- They read no disk: `cache.snapshot()` copies only `shared_ptr`s.
- Each (series, revision) is encoded once, and every subscriber with no pending finals shares the same bytes.
- Sort the open record's entries before encoding. They come from a `pmr::unordered_map`, and `validate()` requires sorted keys.
- Give raw-tail subscriptions their own cap, separate from the existing 64-subscription limit.

**Backpressure:** each session gets a second `LiveWriteSlot`, at most 1 MiB, independent of the legacy slot.
- If a send is refused while the slot is busy, the next send carries the latest state.
- The cadence backs off.
- The final marker stays in place, so finals ride along on the next frame that is accepted.

**Reconnect:** the client re-subscribes after `connected()` plus fresh availability, and passes `since_ms`.
- The server resends the finals it still holds (`LiveCache` keeps the last 16).
- Anything older arrives through the chunk path.

**Handover:** when a frame's `committedThroughMs` is ahead of the stored open chunk, the client triggers an immediate `have_hash` revalidation instead of waiting for the availability push.

## 3. Client

**Buffer (S5L-b):** live data does not enter `ChunkStore`. Revising the open chunk at 1 Hz would rebuild the whole span every second (105-132 ms in S5c).
- Instead, `ChunkFetcher` owns a small `LiveEdge` per (symbol, source), on the heatmap-data thread, with one subscription per symbol for the whole process.
- It holds the minute columns at or after the store's open-chunk `committedThroughMs`, its proven range, and the latest revision.
- It drops minutes once the chunk covers them, and emits `liveChanged(symbol)`.
- The transport gains `subscribeLive` / `unsubscribeLive` and a `liveReceived` signal.
- `SentinelStreamClient` routes kind 2 to its own signal and skips `acceptChunkOrder`.

**No double count:** a minute comes from the chunk if its start is before the chunk's `committedThroughMs`, and from the live buffer otherwise, never from both. A hole (lost finals) stays NotLoaded and draws the loading hatch, never zero.

**Controller:** per chart, it composes a live window `[L, openEnd)` at the chart's timeframe and publishes it as a separate `LiveSnapshot` (`latestLive()`), so the node does not re-index spans every second.
- Committed minutes come from the store; minutes after the chunk's cutoff come from `LiveEdge`.
- `TimeComposer` gains a "forming" option; today it drops incomplete buckets.
- Fully committed columns are cached per chunk generation, so each update recomposes only the forming bucket, on the build pool, with the latest update winning.
- `L` is the lowest complete end among the live-edge span's builds that may still be drawn. It advances only after the node reports the new build uploaded.
- The window's resolution columns merge into the summary used for Auto tick.

**Node (S5L-c):** a live slot per source, using the fill-pass rules (coarsest source first, then fill).
- Upload and bin happen only when the revision changes: one pass over 1-3 columns, covering the rows around the view.
- Draw clip:
  - the drawn span bin (current or fallback) draws buckets before its complete end `E`;
  - the live bin draws `[max(L, E), openEnd)`;
  - if `E < L`, the gap draws the loading hatch.
- Every bucket comes from exactly one bin, and values are identical anywhere two bins overlap.

**Partial column:** a cell is the time-weighted average over the time covered so far (spec rule 5).
- Inside the minute bounds, rows are observed, so a zero is a real zero.
- Rows not covered for the whole observed time draw the veil.
- Time when the book was invalid is excluded, never filled.
- The forming column has no special marker (owner decision 2).

**Transitions:** the live bin is an ordinary entry in `now_`, `held_` and the fading set.
- A timeframe switch holds the old timeframe's live bin together with the held picture.
- A tick change bins the live window at the new tick in the same frame, and the old one fades with its layer.
- A new revision replaces the bin without a crossfade.

**Minute rollover never blanks:** a pending minute stays in the buffer until the chunk covers it and a span bin containing it is being drawn.

**Disconnected** (owner decision 3): the last forming column stays drawn, frozen, and the existing connection indicator shows the disconnect.

## 4. Threading and cost bounds

- **Recorder:** unchanged.
- **LiveService worker:** about 1-2 ms per series update (sort and zstd), with reused scratch buffers.
- **GUI thread:** reads a pointer only.
- **Render thread:** per revision, about 8 bytes per entry, roughly 50-200 KB per source for 1m, up to about 1 MiB for the forming 1D bucket. This fits the existing 8 MiB-per-frame budget, with 1-2 compute passes.
- **No `QRhiBuffer` churn in steady state:** live buffers keep their capacity and are refilled in place; the binner gains a "reuse in place" call.
- **Frames with no new revision** do no live work.
- **1h / 4h / 1D charts** also update at 1 Hz, with the committed part cached (owner decision 4). Fall back to 5 s only if composing measures above 5 ms.

## S5L-a implementation notes

- Kind 2 retains the SHC1 v2 native body and adds an explicit signed i64 `tailStartMs` after header `endMs`. The chunk key still names the open minute's hour; using the key start as the body start would lose pending minutes across an hour boundary.
- Scan claims are conservative: coalesced ranges of transported records only. Unlike a disk scan, a bounded mailbox cannot distinguish a recorder gap from an evicted final. Omitted minutes stay NotLoaded and are resolved by chunks. This refines the section 2 scan wording without inventing empty history.
- A commit can arrive before the next open publication. The inferred open minute is the later of the commit cutoff and the newest provisional start; an unobserved open minute has no synthetic column.
- Raw service capacity is 128 symbol subscriptions, independent of the legacy 64-view cap. The per-session limit remains eight. The independent raw 1 MiB aggregate byte budget admits multiple sibling frames in FIFO order; attempts rotate after budget pressure to prevent starvation.
- Encoding variants are keyed by the first held final needed in a revision; subscribers needing the same suffix share bytes, including one shared variant for every subscriber with no pending finals. Only an accepted transport admission advances a final marker.
- Private clock/worker-turn test seams exercise the real live worker and TLS transport deterministically. The recorder and its publication throttle are unchanged.
- The client wire adapter exposes subscriptions and rejects stale replies; reconnect orchestration, LiveEdge, and immediate chunk revalidation remain S5L-b. The recording-only local transport explicitly reports live unsupported (id 0).

S5L-a validation: the deterministic codec, raw-worker, transport-adapter, and TLS tests cover sorted entries, shared encodings, final markers, re-subscribe replay, 1 Hz cadence, the 2/4/5 s busy backoff, symbol limits, unsubscribe, epoch invalidation, and live teardown. Mutation checks removed accepted final-marker advancement (the finals-once test failed), advanced that marker on refused frames (both worker and TLS coalescing tests failed with lost finals), and bypassed kind-2 state validation (the valid-hash malformed-frame test failed). All mutations were restored.

Cost check: `recording_live_bench --raw 30`, 12,000 shuffled entries/minute, measures sort/conversion, validation, hash and zstd without disk/TLS. With immutable pending-column reuse, zero/two pending minutes measured 0.64-0.88 ms/update; 60 pending minutes measured 6.53-6.62 ms/update. One and eight subscribers both encoded once per revision. The maximum-lateness stress case exceeds the approximate 1-2 ms target; the common fixture meets it. This is synthetic CPU evidence, not a live recorder latency or GUI measurement.

## S5L-a review fixes (on top of 010d77f)

Review-authorized refinements to section 2:
- Replace the single raw `LiveWriteSlot` with an aggregate 1 MiB byte budget (including envelopes). Both sources and several symbols can deliver every second; queued raw frames are FIFO. A full budget still coalesces, backs off, and preserves final markers.
- Preserve recorder flags. During rollover the newest known provisional can be a complete, lateness-held minute; do not synthesize `kPartial` or require it on the last column. The frame extent follows the latest known minute until the next open publication.
- Consume empty revisions without congestion backoff. Catch allocations throughout raw worker collection, state, snapshot and encoding, log failures, and retry with cadence backoff.
- Log oversized tails with throttled symbol/source/byte diagnostics and trim oldest pending minutes to fit. Preserve the newest minute and an included prefix of finals; if finals need trimming, drop their newest suffix to avoid advancing a high-water marker past an omitted final. Omitted minutes have no scan claim and fall back to chunks. An indivisible oversized minute can be omitted entirely.
- Advertise `chunk_live` only when recording is available and the live service exists. Check live final conversion against `fromRecording`, including filtered entries that affect the native base row.
- Document the at-most-17 encoding variants per series/revision. The existing SHE1-to-owning-string double copy remains; the budget accounts for wire bytes, not transient copies.

These changes do not alter recorder behavior, publication throttle, or the S5L-b client/controller scope.

Review-fix validation: the TLS test changes both sources on three symbols every injected 1-second turn and parks the socket executor to assert six simultaneous admissions and FIFO delivery. Worker tests cover rollover flags, empty revision consumption, aggregate byte accounting, oversized-tail delivery with warning/scan/marker checks, `fromRecording` parity, the 17-variant bound, and injected `bad_alloc` at collection/state/snapshot boundaries with recovery at 2/4-second backoff. Capability tests cover all availability/service combinations.

Fail-without-fix spot checks restored the single-frame admission restriction, reversed raw FIFO, forced rollover `kPartial`, restored the codec's last-column partial rule, treated empty builds as refusals, bypassed trimming, retained a trimmed final's marker, suppressed the oversize warning, advertised live unconditionally, removed conversion base-row parity, bypassed the documented variant sharing, and rethrew raw allocation failures. Each corresponding test failed (the allocation fault aborted its isolated test process with uncaught `bad_alloc`); all mutations were restored. The variant-cache comment itself is documentation; the cache-bypass check verifies its stated bound/sharing behavior.

## S5L-b implementation notes (2026-09-30)

- `ChunkFetcher::wantLive(chart, symbol)` / `releaseLive(chart)` share one subscription over all advertised minute sources. The chart wants it only when its view intersects the live bucket. Subscription ids gate frames; reconnect waits for fresh availability and uses the minimum stored open-chunk cutoff across sources. The old picture freezes until a new-epoch frame arrives, which accepts a lower server revision and replaces old uncommitted observations. Chart id 0 is reserved for the fetcher's tail chunk wants.
- Core `LiveEdge` retains immutable minute pointers, a coalesced proven range, and server revision plus a client-local version. Equal pending columns reuse their pointers, including the just-closed newest provisional; no forming/pending inference depends on `kPartial`. Individual columns own their storage, rather than retaining a whole decoded frame per historical revision. Stored cutoffs trim the buffer; the worker independently enforces chunk ownership before live ownership, covering the interval before a queued trim is delivered. An ahead frame revalidates wanted minute chunks immediately with `have_hash`; another advance during that request causes a follow-up when necessary.
- Core `LiveComposer` caches chunk contributions by generation, unchanged leading contributions to each bucket, and finished output buckets by immutable input pointers. It combines unquantized numerators, so recomposition does not round codes twice. Same-minute updates recompose only the changing bucket; an actual commit/generation change or a changed pending minute invalidates its affected cache. Missing minutes remain NotLoaded. `TimeComposer::ComposeOptions::forming` proves only the last scanned prefix (not a fixed chunk's bounding end); default history composition is unchanged.
- `HeatmapSourceController::latestLive()` returns a separate immutable `LiveSnapshot`; `liveChanged()` tells consumers to take it. Each source has `[startMs, openEndMs)`, composed columns, a CPU `GpuSource` upload image, and server revision. Use **LiveSnapshot.version**, not the server revision, as the upload identity: a restart can lower the server revision, and chunk handover or L can change the image without a new wire revision. Its `serial` follows symbol/timeframe/store resets. The node must retain old snapshot pointers with held/fading pictures in S5L-c.
- `SpanSourceBuild::completeEndMs` supplies E. Published replacements preserve the lower E until the existing capacity report acknowledges their upload; all sources share the lowest L. The complete-end fallback for a wholly forming 1D bucket uses the local committed cutoff rounded to the timeframe, not the start of a 64-day tile. This prevents an initial daily view from requesting many weeks of minute chunks.
- Auto integration refinement: **`latestResolution()`** is a thread-safe immutable summary merging `SpanSet::resolution` with the latest live resolution. S5L-c must read that accessor for Auto. Updating `SpanSet::resolution` itself every second would replace the SpanSet pointer and make the existing node re-index spans, defeating the separate live publication. The historical SpanSet pointer stays identical during ordinary live updates.
- Live jobs use the same bounded `SpanSourceCache` pool and chunk ledger as span jobs; there is at most one running live job per chart. New inputs coalesce, stale completions cannot publish, and destruction/serial changes do not let a worker touch a controller. Retained live cache, columns, image and resolution bytes join the chart's CPU commitment. The live snapshot's CPU image stays available between revisions for S5L-c re-upload after GPU loss.
- All timeframes start at 1000 ms. `heatmap.live.compose` reports complete worker update cost (composition, image preparation and summary), bucket/prefix counts and interval. A measured update above 5 ms changes the next interval to 5000 ms with a warning; a subsequent update at or below 5 ms restores 1000 ms. Measuring the complete CPU update is conservative relative to measuring composition alone. The injected 6 ms clock test exercises backoff deterministically; the small real-clock fixture did not trigger it (warm updates approximately 0.06-0.20 ms; 1D initial preparation approximately 2.1 ms). These are synthetic CPU measurements, not full-book or live recorder latency claims.

Validation covers FakeChunkTransport handover interleavings (including a lost final and composition before queued trimming), hour rollover, the LiveSnapshot/SpanSet coverage union, delayed upload and L, source cutoff differences, one subscription across two charts and last release, reconnect since_ms/epoch/revision reset, immediate hash revalidation and an advance during an outstanding request, latest-wins jobs, 1 Hz through 1D, prefix/bucket recomposition counts, a fixed bounding chunk extent versus its proven forming cutoff, and interior holes. The 1m/5m/1h CPU bin oracle agrees with composing all raw minutes; a deterministic BookRecorder replay also agrees with LiveBuilder display codes and analytical TWAP at 1m/5m.
