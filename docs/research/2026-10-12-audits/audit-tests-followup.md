## Section C: What the suite protects

Counts are **source-declared gtest cases**, not executed cases or ctest entries: **1,624 total, including five disabled cases**. They exclude standalone harnesses and duplicate ctest invocations. “Gap” below identifies a limitation observed in this audit, not a claim of exhaustive coverage analysis.

**`tests/agentapi` — 81 cases.** Protects request validation, chart control/state reporting, safe hosted settings, dock operations, and symbol-switch acknowledgement/refusal. Gap: the new stored/drawn/completed-revision test manually supplies the drawn frame and completion acknowledgement; it does not itself prove that an actual GPU frame and HTTP response agree.

**`tests/capture` — 123 cases.** Protects exact raw-message recording, corruption detection, rotation, bounded queues, product isolation, reconnects, fan-out and shutdown. Gap: controlled transports and child-process fixtures do not establish correct operation against the current live provider; several process cases are Unix-only.

**`tests/core` — 21 cases.** Protects logging filters/rate limits, log retention, metric counters, metric formatting and the HTTP metrics endpoint. Gap: the log-sink test exercises successful writes and pruning, without a failing-write case for a full or unwritable destination.

**`tests/fonts` — 10 cases.** Protects font persistence, propagation into existing/hidden widgets, and preserving user-selected column widths. Gap: screenshot fixtures save images but do not automatically fail for clipped or unreadable text.

**`tests/link` — 3 cases, plus a symbol-inspection ctest entry.** Protects catching standard exceptions across compiled-library boundaries and detects duplicated standard exception type information. No additional concrete behavioural gap was established here; results remain platform/build dependent and were not rerun.

**`tests/market_health` — 13 cases.** Protects stale/live/unknown status, reconnect requirements, rejection of obsolete responses, and stopping hidden-widget polling. Biggest gap: three changed cases now have the Qt-versus-GoogleTest failure-reporting problem in B.

**`tests/marketdata` — 96 cases.** Protects message parsing, side conversion, subscriptions, connection retries, bounded attempts, serialized writes and order-book operations. Gap: the schema-family cases do not exercise family-specific receiving code; the footprint-size case does not test actual payload-length rejection.

**`tests/render` — 729 cases, one disabled, plus standalone view-trace and GPU-golden harnesses.** Protects chart coordinates, zoom/pan/follow/fit behaviour, symbol/timeframe changes, heatmap data preparation, GPU resource lifetime, overlay geometry, controls and settings. Biggest concrete gap: visible bubbles appearing after an initially empty overlay receives trades—the golden deliberately avoids that sequence.

**`tests/research_ui` — 14 cases.** Protects stale-result rejection, identity checks, missing financial values, process cancellation, child cleanup and output limits. Gap: fake provider responses and screenshot saving do not validate current external-provider formats or automatically catch visual defects; several process checks skip on Windows.

**`tests/roller` — 134 cases, one disabled.** Protects journal replay, checkpoint/anchor recovery, corruption handling, durable-versus-provisional data, isolation between products, outage recovery, trade deduplication and candle continuity. Gap: default runs do not exercise the opt-in captured-hour/day datasets; the two latency benchmarks print slowdowns without failing on them.

**`tests/servermodel` — 337 cases, three disabled.** Protects recording values/formats, disk faults, historical queries, caches, live delivery, admission, metrics and server lifecycle. Biggest concrete gap: `ServerStartup.MissingTlsLeavesTheRequiredRecorderRunning` does not prove a new record is written while TLS startup fails.

**`tests/trading` — 63 cases.** Protects order/position accounting, fills, cancellation, attached risk exits, algorithm callbacks and deterministic replay; new cases meaningfully test rejection of non-finite numbers. Gap: the new legacy-CLI refusal test can reject an invocation for missing arguments rather than the condition named by the test.

**`tests/support` — zero cases; 530 lines of reference implementation/header.** Supplies the older recording projection as a comparison reference for current heatmap code. Its presence supports independent comparison, but agreement between two implementations is not proof of correctness; retain independently calculated examples too.

The top-level deployment script is a separate shell test, not included in the gtest count; it checks deployment through a dry run, not an actual service replacement.

- These figures **exclude deletions already landed**, the existing `-j 4` improvement, speculative deletion of lab coverage, and any change to the required full landing gate.
